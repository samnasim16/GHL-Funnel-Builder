# Pitch: FunnelForge for BAD Marketing

**To:** Eddie Maalouf, Founder & CEO, BAD Marketing

## The short version

I didn't send a portfolio link. I built the tool I'd use on the job and loaded it with five funnels for BAD's own service lines and client types. Each funnel comes with the GoHighLevel back end that makes it convert: pipeline, workflows, speed-to-lead, show-up reminders and no-show recovery.

## Why this matters to BAD

Most funnel builders hand over a pretty page. A funnel that makes money also needs:

1. **Speed to lead**: an SMS in under 60 seconds and a rep notified with the lead's revenue band.
2. **Qualification**: senior strategists only see $1M+/mo brands. Everyone else gets nurtured, not ignored.
3. **Show rate**: confirmation, then reminders at 24h, 2h and 10m. Every reminder gives a reason to show up.
4. **Recovery**: abandoned-application and no-show flows that rebook leads already paid for.
5. **Attribution**: UTMs and click IDs stored on the contact, so cost per *booked call* can be reported per ad.

Every template in FunnelForge ships all five, written out as GHL workflows with the actual message copy.
